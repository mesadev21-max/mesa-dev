01 — Philosophy

Nothing from further than the coastline can see

We built the kitchen around a single rule: if it didn't grow, swim, or graze within reach of the fjord, it doesn't reach the table. That means a shorter menu, and a longer relationship with the seven foragers, two fishermen, and one dairy we work with.

03
Open kitchen, open book
Ask where anything came from — we'll usually know the farmer's name.
